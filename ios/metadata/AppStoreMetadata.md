## 2. Description (en-US)
Personal AI Hub is an intelligent life aggregator that unifies your independent productivity and finance ecosystems into a single, cohesive interface.

Features:
- **Daily AI Briefing:** Real-time synthesis of monthly spending limits and overdue task deadlines.
- **Smart Bento Grid:** Quick-glance summaries from F-Tracker and Task Management apps.
- **Interactive AI Assistant:** Conversational query answering powered by Google Gemini with safe, authenticated deep link actions.
- **Account Mismatch Guard:** Intercepts cross-app navigation to ensure you are always viewing records for the right profile.
